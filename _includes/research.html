Research

Most models learn from outcomes, which tell them whether they succeeded but not why. My work gives models richer signals across the three abilities an intelligent agent needs. To reason, a language model learns where to look, not just which answer is right. To perceive, it fuses modalities so that one view fills in what another misses. To act, a robot learns why it failed, as a vision–language model turns its mistakes into rewards.

01 · Reason

Reinforcement Learning & Post-Training

Outcome rewards only say whether an answer was right. I distill a solution-aware teacher's attention into the student, so it learns where to look and reasons more accurately with far shorter rollouts.

Each modality sees part of the picture. I fuse complementary views, from video and skeletons to spectra, text and occluded scenes, into representations that hold up when a single view fails.
