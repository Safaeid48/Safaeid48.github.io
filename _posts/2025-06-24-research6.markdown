A Qwen-2.5VL-3B–driven episodic reflection module was designed to generate natural-language self-assessments of each trial, enabling human-like introspection. These reflections were fused with CLIP-style vision–language feedback from task descriptions and goal images to construct a dense, semantically grounded reward model. Integrated into a reward-aligned Soft Actor–Critic pipeline, this enriched feedback loop accelerated exploration, reduced training time, and improved policy convergence across Meta-World manipulation tasks.
