Safaeid Hossain Arib

Machine Learning Engineer & Researcher

Hello, I am Safaeid Hossain Arib, a Machine Learning Engineer at ACI Limited. My work focuses on translating advances in machine learning into dependable systems for real-world applications. Previously, I worked as an AI Engineer at Ontik Technology, developing agentic AI and applied machine learning solutions.

My current research explores how language models can learn where to attend through privileged-query attention distillation. I conduct this work under the guidance of Dr. Md. Mofijul Islam and Dr. Tasnim Mohiuddin.

I hold a B.Sc. in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Dhaka. After completing my degree, I worked with Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan at the AVIS Research Lab on an open-world amodal counting framework. During my undergraduate studies, I worked with Dr. Shafin Rahman and Dr. Sejuti Rahman on continuous sign language translation.

When I’m not coding or reading research papers, you’ll likely find me on the basketball court, lifting at the gym, or off exploring new places. I love connecting with curious minds—feel free to reach out!

I am actively seeking MS/PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.
