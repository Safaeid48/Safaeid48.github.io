We propose a novel three-stage framework that leverages Tri-Modal Encoding, Adaptive Multi-Head Cross-Modal Alignment, and Channel-Wise Residual Connections to enhance time series forecasting. By integrating frequency, temporal, and language-model-based representations, our method achieves richer feature fusion and state-of-the art performance in several time series forecasting datasets.
