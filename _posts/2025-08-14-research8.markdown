<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Millions of people who are deaf or hard of hearing communicate through sign language, yet in societies built around spoken language it is often under-supported, leading to communication barriers and social exclusion. Most sign language translation systems rely on <em>gloss</em> annotations (word-level transcriptions of each sign), which are slow and expensive to produce, and are especially scarce for Bangla Sign Language.</p>
<p>This work, developed as our undergraduate thesis, builds a gloss-free translation system that fuses a transformer with an STGCN-LSTM architecture, explores several fusion strategies, and evaluates across four sign languages: German (RWTH-PHOENIX-2014T), Chinese (CSL-Daily), American (How2Sign), and Bangla (BornilDB v1.0).</p>

<h2>Method</h2>
<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Appearance stream</strong>I3D features from RGB frames are encoded by a transformer, capturing high-level context across the video.</li>
  <li><strong>Skeleton stream</strong>Pose keypoints form a spatio-temporal graph; an STGCN models joint relationships and an LSTM models their evolution over time.</li>
  <li><strong>Fusion and decoding</strong>The two encodings are fused and a transformer decoder generates the sentence directly, with no intermediate gloss supervision.</li>
</ol>
