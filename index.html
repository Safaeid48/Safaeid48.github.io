Machine Learning Engineer & Researcher

Safaeid Hossain Arib

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at ACI PLC, where I build machine learning systems that run in the real world. Before that, I was an AI Engineer at Ontik Technology, developing agentic AI applications.

My latest first-author work, OPASD, teaches reasoning language models where to look. This work is supervised by Dr. Md Mofijul Islam (Amazon GenAI, USA) and Dr. Tasnim Mohiuddin (Qatar Computing Research Institute, Qatar). I am also working with Dr. Ser-Nam Lim (University of Central Florida, USA) on world models.

I earned my B.Sc. in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Dhaka. After graduating, I worked on amodal counting with Dr. Md Mehedi Hasan (University of Dhaka, Bangladesh) at the AVIS Research Lab. As an undergraduate, I worked on sign language translation with Dr. Shafin Rahman (North South University, Bangladesh) and Dr. Sejuti Rahman (New Uzbekistan University, Uzbekistan).

Outside of research, I play basketball, train at the gym, and travel whenever I can. I am always glad to meet curious people, so please feel free to reach out.

I am actively seeking MS/PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.
